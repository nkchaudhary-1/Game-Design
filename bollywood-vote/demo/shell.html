Best Dressed, from guest list to winner

Both screens below are the real app. The host screen is on the left, a guest's phone on the right. Here they talk through this page; on the real site the phones connect straight to the host's browser. Nothing leaves this page, and a refresh starts over.

Try it

  1. On the host screen, click Fill with 20 sample guests, then Save details, then Go to Best Dressed Award.
  2. Choose 2 minutes or 5 minutes and click Start voting. The countdown starts on every screen.
  3. On the phone, tap your name, then "I'm …", then vote. Nobody sees a score: the host only sees names light up.
  4. Click Dummy guests join, then Dummy guests vote to fill the room.
  5. When the clock hits zero (or on Close voting now), voting closes. Click Reveal Winner and watch the curtain.
  6. For a tie: New award night, start again, then use Make it a tie in place of Dummy guests vote, leave the phone unvoted, close and reveal. You get joint winners, and an optional tie-break.
