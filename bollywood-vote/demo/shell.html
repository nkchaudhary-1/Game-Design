Best Dressed, live voting

Both screens below are the real app, running inside this page with 20 made-up guests. Nothing leaves this page, and a refresh starts over.

Try it

  1. On the projector, click Try it with 20 sample names, then Save guest list. On the night you type your own.
  2. Click Start voting. The QR code and a 5-minute clock appear.
  3. On the phone, tap your name, then "I'm …", then vote. You can change your vote until the clock runs out.
  4. Click Dummy guests join, then Dummy guests vote to fill the room. Voting closes by itself when time is up or everyone has voted.
  5. On the projector, click Reveal winner and watch the curtain.
  6. To see a tie-break, restart, then use Make it a tie in place of Dummy guests vote. Leave the phone unvoted, or vote for someone else.
