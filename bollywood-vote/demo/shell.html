Best Dressed, live voting

Both screens below are the real app, running inside this page with 20 made-up guests. Nothing leaves this page, and a refresh starts over.

Try it

  1. On the phone, tap a name, then "I'm …".
  2. Click Dummy guests join and watch the names light up on the projector.
  3. On the projector, click Open voting.
  4. On the phone, pick someone and vote. You can change your vote until voting closes.
  5. Click Dummy guests vote to fill the room.
  6. On the projector, click Close voting, then Reveal winner.
